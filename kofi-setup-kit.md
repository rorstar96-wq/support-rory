## 5. Payment setup

- Ko-fi payouts go to **PayPal** or **Stripe** (Stripe = straight to your bank, better for AU).
  Choose **Stripe** if you have a bank account — lower effective fees and no PayPal middleman.
- If Stripe isn't available for your setup, PayPal works — you'll need a PayPal account connected.
- Ko-fi takes **0%** on donations. Only the payment processor fee applies (~2-3%).
