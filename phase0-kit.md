## 2. First 3 Reddit warm-up comments (age the account, days 1–3)

Don't post these word-for-word — read the thread and adjust so you sound like you, not a template. These are shapes:

**Shape A — answer a pain question (r/ChronicPain):**
> Mine's nerve pain from a shunt + a bad infection a few years back — 6/10 baseline, worse as the day goes. The thing that helped me most wasn't a med, it was accepting the day has a budget and planning around it. Mornings are my good hours, so I front-load everything that matters. You might have a different budget — but finding yours is half the battle.

**Shape B — respond to a "does anyone else" post:**
> Yes — and you're not imagining it. [relate to their specific thing in one sentence]. You're not lazy, you're running a harder race with less in the tank.

**Shape C — welcome a newcomer:**
> Welcome. This place helped me more than any doctor appointment, honestly — a room where nobody has to explain themselves. Take what's useful, ignore the rest.

**Rules of thumb for warm-up week:** comment 2–3×/day max, never mention your page, never link anything. Just be a person. By day 3–4 you'll have karma and the filters will trust you.
