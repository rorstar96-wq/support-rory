The story

At four years old, I caught Rasmussen's encephalitis — a rare condition that was destroying one half of my brain. The only way to stop it was to take that half out. I had a hemispherectomy: I've lived my whole life with half a brain, a weaker right side, and half a field of vision.

In 2017, a brain shunt and a near-fatal MRSA infection added chronic nerve pain to the list. My baseline is a six out of ten, every day, and it gets worse as the day goes on.

I'm not telling you this for sympathy. I'm telling you because it's the price of admission, and I've never once let it stop me from showing up. I trained as a counsellor and coach.
